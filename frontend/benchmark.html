NAICS with Jev

Benchmark on real company descriptions

Classifier

60 real company descriptions, reviewed case by case

A random sample of unique descriptions from the public ExioNAICS dataset (Hugging Face), each run live through the hybrid classifier (search, tree walk only when search is unsure), on the pinned Jev model. A separate reviewer judged every answer blind, from the description and the official Census text, without seeing the classifier's probabilities.

The 4 arguable picks are all the same kind of call: wholesale vs retail, or franchisor vs operator. 4 of the 5 missed holds leaned to the right code but stayed under the bar.

The public labels are less reliable than the classifier

Reviewing the dataset's own NAICS labels for the same 60 descriptions:

19labels right
23partly right (one of several codes)
18labels wrong

Scored against the labels alone, only 27 of the 40 assigned codes look right. On review, all 13 others hold up: 10 defensible, 3 arguable. That is why each case is judged on its merits, not by label match.

Examples

  • Assigned A roofing contractor in Rochester, NY

    Jev: 238160 Roofing Contractors. Dataset label: 111310 Orange Groves.

    Defensible; the label is wrong.

  • Assigned Geomechanics consulting for oil and gas reservoirs

    Jev: 541330 Engineering Services. Dataset label: 513210 Software Publishers.

    Defensible; the label is wrong.

  • Assigned A hotel brand company that franchises its brands to owners

    Jev: 533110 Lessors of Nonfinancial Intangible Assets (franchising). Dataset labels: 531390, 721110 Hotels.

    Defensible: the business earns franchise royalties, it does not mainly run hotels.

  • Held A freight forwarder: land, air and ocean freight, contract logistics

    Jev held it for a person. Its lean was 488510 Freight Transportation Arrangement, which matches the label.

    A safe miss: it should have acted, and a person sees it instead of a wrong code.

How to read this

  • A hold is the design: when the description does not settle a code, a person decides instead of a guess.
  • On a hold, the code Jev leaned to is a diagnostic, not a suggestion. Some reasonable holds leaned the wrong way.
  • 60 cases is a small sample. Thresholds are calibrated separately and were not tuned on these cases.
